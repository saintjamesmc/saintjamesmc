Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the manner in which the Minecraft Whitelist and Discord Integration Service (the "Service") collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by users.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to all users who interact with the Service, including but not limited to those who submit verification requests, link Discord accounts, or access the associated Minecraft server.

2. Information Collected

The Service collects limited information necessary for identity verification and access control.

2.1 Collected Information

  • Email address used for verification
  • Minecraft account identifier (Java username or Bedrock gamertag)
  • Minecraft platform designation (Java or Bedrock)
  • Discord user ID obtained through Discord account linking
  • Community, class, or group association derived from authorized records
  • Date and time of verification and whitelist approval

2.2 Information Not Collected

The Service does not intentionally collect:

  • Passwords
  • Financial or payment information
  • Personal mailing addresses
  • Discord account email addresses
  • Sensitive personal identifiers

3. Method of Collection

Information is collected when users:

  • Submit a verification email to the Service
  • Link a Discord account through DiscordSRV
  • Receive approval for Minecraft server access

4. Purpose of Processing

Information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Verifying eligibility for server access
  • Preventing unauthorized or duplicate access
  • Whitelisting users on the Minecraft server
  • Assigning Discord roles and in-game teams
  • Administrative auditing and troubleshooting

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • Data is stored in a locally hosted SQLite database (links.db)
  • The database is not publicly accessible
  • Information is retained only for the duration necessary to maintain access control and administrative records

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

The Service does not sell, rent, or otherwise distribute user information to third parties.

Information may be shared only with:

  • The Minecraft server for whitelist management
  • Discord services for role and account integration

7. Data Modification and Removal

  • Users may request replacement of their linked Minecraft account by confirming such request via email
  • Administrators may remove or modify records as required
  • Data may be deleted when access is revoked or no longer required

8. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are implemented to protect stored data. However, no system can guarantee complete security, and use of the Service is at the user's own risk.

9. Children and Minors

The Service is intended for use within a private or institutional community. No data is collected for commercial purposes, profiling, or marketing.

10. Policy Modifications

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of any changes.

11. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy should be directed to a Service administrator.
